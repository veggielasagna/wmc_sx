# WMC SX privacy policy

WMC SX does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- The app plays music, videos and photos from media servers on your own home network (DLNA) and from USB drives connected to your console.
- It connects only to devices on your local network. It does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.
- Your settings, library index and "continue watching" positions are saved only on your console, inside the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/veggielasagna/wmc_sx/issues
